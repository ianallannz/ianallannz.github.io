Proposing a qualification in resilient technologies #

Preamble #

So much for being part-time in my main role this year to give space to other ideas. In February I returned to full-time through additional union advocacy and fill-in teaching. Add in my great dad passing, coaching three football teams, and an active household... life has been full.

Still, by sticking to 5am starts — a couple of hours of clear air each day before morning madness — and a recent return to part-time, I've managed to get some things together.

Saved, and a Diploma in Resilient Technologies #

Everything I've read, listened to, ruminated, and experienced over the last four years has funnelled through my professional and personal world view to create Saved. And now a Diploma in Resilient Technologies:

  • Pitch for a new Diploma in Resilient Technologies
  • Full proposal for a new Diploma in Resilient Technologies

Trying to follow through #

After reasonably failing to become a local councillor last year, something I said was:

If we accept that the broader system is making things worse for our rangatahi — if we fall in love with the problem — then we can just get on with solving things for ourselves out here.

Now is the time to build our own resiliency, build our own self-sufficiency ... and support and invest in each other.

I'm trying to follow through on that call-to-arms at Saved.
