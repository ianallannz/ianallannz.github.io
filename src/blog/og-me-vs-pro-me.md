## Some people don't like Me

That's understandable* 

Fortunately now you can also have an inoffensive Pro version of Me!

There's a switch right there in the navigation bar. So if you don't like Me, you can choose a **generically professional version of Ian**, or back to the **uniquely me version of Ian**.

Appropriately, I used AI to get me started with a *"typically modern professional"* design style. Because that's we all want eh? To churn out derivative works that look fine, like the other derivative works flooding the zone. 

Whatever it is you need &mdash; the professionally ordinary me, or the more extraordinary, the more challenging me &mdash; I can surely help.
