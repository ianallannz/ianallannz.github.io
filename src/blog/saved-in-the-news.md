## On the up: Saving computers from my garden shed!

It was a pleasure to have Zoe come and visit after she'd heard about what I was up to from a satisfied Saved customer. 

I really appreciate the story she picked out of everything I had to say about big tech lock-in, perilous futures for our young people, connecting to the new cyberpunks, building a kitchen out of 70s rimu remnants...

[Saved: Old laptops get new life](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/gisborne-herald/news/on-the-up-gisborne-e-waste-project-repurposes-old-laptops-for-local-community/BOL55LPQ3RCFJNX7OXWSDPGUFU/)

Though I did get caught short with the story being published while I was away with a [Campion football team at AIMS Games](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdAoQJgBPkp/). So I'm really appreciative of The Environment Centre having a supply of laptop chargers, just the right ones too. Legends.

Meanwhile, you'll see over at [Saved](www.saved.co.nz) that I'm urgently seeking interest from businesses sitting on old laptops that could support the initiative.

### And what's this about putting a price on them?

One of the [risks](https://saved.co.nz/prop/#risks) I identified when first coming up with Saved, was that the business model could be considered a self-centred, for-profit competitor in the ecosystem. I sense this risk is bearing out to some extent. 

I'm looking forward to talking to groups in the near future about the business of Saving laptops; about the value of a $ and how and when it's earned: before you start or after you've created enough value for an end user.
